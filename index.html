About her

Born in Isfahan in 1984, raised in Tehran, painting professionally since eighteen.

Fariba Rahnavard

Painter, PhD in the history of art

She studied painting at the Islamic Azad University in Tehran, then turned to abstract expressionism, drawn to the tragedies of the modern world.

Disaster Never Rests

Solo exhibitions, Tehran 2018 and Isfahan 2019

She builds her images in layers of photograph, paint and paper, and counts Francis Bacon, Marina Abramović and René Magritte among her influences.

Gozaar Gallery, Isfahan

Solo exhibition, April 2019

Her work has been shown at international biennials and exhibitions, including the Florence Biennale and the Manhattan International Competition.

Kolaj Magazine

Featured artist, 2021

Her collages have also been reviewed in ARTiculAction in London and selected by Lunch Ticket in Los Angeles. She has taught painting since 2004.
