About

Fariba Rahnavard is a modern and contemporary Iranian painter, born in Isfahan in 1984 and raised in Tehran.

She began painting professionally at eighteen and later studied painting at the Faculty of Art and Architecture of the Islamic Azad University, Central Tehran Branch, before completing a PhD in the history of art in Spain.

Drawn to the tragedies of the modern world, she turned to abstract expressionism, a way of painting long tied to history, violence and catastrophe. She builds her images in layers of photograph, paint and paper, and counts Francis Bacon, Marina Abramović and René Magritte among her influences.

Her work has been shown at international biennials and exhibitions, including the Florence Biennale and the Manhattan International Competition. She has taught painting since 2004.
